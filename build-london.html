Build london.json

Hidden page for Fed for a Fiver. It asks OpenStreetMap (Overpass) for every food shop, café, pub and supermarket in Greater London, keeps the ones that belong to the 103 chains, places the uni bars, markets and independents, checks the test list of 138 branches and gives you london.json to download.

Takes about 10 minutes. Leave this tab open and in front. Overpass and Nominatim are free public services: if one is busy the page waits and tries again.
